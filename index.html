Operating Procedure

Master the Electric Circuits Lab in five simple steps. Follow this visual guide to effortlessly build, simulate, and analyze your circuits.

🖱️

1. Adding Components

Use the Draw menu at the top of the screen (or simply right-click anywhere on the blank canvas) to select a component. Click and drag your mouse across the canvas to place and size the component perfectly.

🔌

2. Wiring the Circuit

Select Add Wire (Shortcut: w). Click and drag from the terminal of one component to the terminal of another to connect them. Always ensure you provide a Ground connection for the simulation to reference 0V.

▶️

3. Running the Simulation

Once your circuit is completely wired, click the prominent green ▶ RUN button at the top left. Watch the simulation come to life as animated dots move along the wires to indicate current flow and direction.

⚙️

4. Editing Values

Need to change resistance, capacitance, or voltage? Simply double-click any placed component. A quick dialog box will appear allowing you to input exact parameters and engineering units instantly.

📊

5. Using the Oscilloscope

Hover your mouse over any active component, right-click, and select View in Scope. A beautiful live waveform graph will appear at the bottom of the screen, tracking the real-time voltage and current of that specific component.
